Why universities pay for FairShare

FairShare reduces hidden staff workload in group modules by replacing ad-hoc chasing, evidence collation, and moderation guesswork with one auditable workflow.

1) Less admin time for teaching staff

Lecturers and tutors spend less time chasing updates across email threads, chat screenshots, and disconnected files. Project status, ownership, and contribution evidence are already in one place before marking week.

  • Fewer manual check-ins per group
  • Less inbox back-and-forth about “who did what”
  • Quicker prep for tutorials and intervention meetings

2) Faster, fairer moderation

When group performance is contested, staff can review timelines, task ownership, and activity signals quickly instead of reconstructing evidence from multiple tools after submission.

  • Contribution logs available at moderation time
  • Clearer basis for differentiated marking decisions
  • Reduced disputes and escalation load for module teams

3) Better student outcomes with earlier intervention

FairShare surfaces low activity and imbalance earlier in the term, so staff can coach groups before final deliverables are at risk — improving completion quality and student satisfaction.

  • Earlier visibility into at-risk groups
  • More targeted support from tutors
  • Fewer end-of-term “surprises” in team projects
Bottom line: keep your LMS for submission and grades, and use FairShare to manage the group-work process that consumes staff time.

FAQ

Educators, students, and procurement — including questions we hear in school procurement conversations.

General

What is FairShare?
FairShare is a platform built for university students working in teams. It tracks who contributes what on a group project, so everyone's work is visible and fairly recognised — not just the loudest voice in the room.
Who is FairShare for?
It's designed for university students who work in groups for assessed coursework. It's also useful for lecturers and tutors who want a clearer picture of how a team is actually functioning, not just what the final submission looks like.
Is FairShare free to use?
FairShare offers a free tier for individual students to get started. Premium plans are available for larger teams and institutional access. Check the pricing page for the latest details.

Tracking & contributions

How does FairShare track contributions?
Each team member logs tasks, updates, and completed work directly in the platform. FairShare pulls this together in real time to show a breakdown of who is doing what — across writing, research, coordination, and other task types.
Can someone game the system by logging fake tasks?
Task logs are visible to the whole team and, where enabled, to your instructor too. This transparency makes it difficult to inflate contributions without others noticing. The platform is built around accountability, not just self-reporting.
What happens if a team member stops contributing?
FairShare flags low or no activity so it doesn't go unnoticed. Team members can raise this within the platform, and instructors can be granted visibility to step in if needed. It creates a paper trail so the issue is documented rather than ignored.

For instructors

Can lecturers and tutors see team progress?
Yes. Instructors can be given dashboard access to monitor team activity, view contribution breakdowns, and track project milestones. This gives a much more accurate picture of group dynamics than a single submitted report.
Can FairShare data be used when marking group work?
That's entirely up to the instructor and their institution's policy. FairShare provides the data — contribution logs, activity timelines, and task completion records — which can inform decisions around differentiated marking if that's supported at your university.

Privacy & data

Who can see my contributions?
By default, your contributions are visible to your own team. Instructor visibility is only enabled if your team or institution sets it up. You always have a clear view of who has access to your data within your project settings.
Is my data stored securely?
FairShare is built on a Supabase backend with real-time data handling and secure authentication. Your project data is stored safely and is only accessible to authorised users within your team.
