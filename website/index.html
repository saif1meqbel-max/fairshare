What is FairShare? +
FairShare is a workspace built for university students working in teams on assessed coursework. It tracks who contributes what, so everyone's work is visible and fairly recognised — and lecturers have the evidence they need at marking time.
Who is FairShare for? +
Primarily for universities and higher education institutions running group coursework modules. It's useful for module leaders, lecturers, and tutors who want visibility into team dynamics — and for students who want transparent credit for their work.
Is FairShare free to use? +
Yes — the Starter tier is free for up to 25 seats and requires no card. Team and Campus tiers are available for larger deployments with additional support and compliance documentation.
Does FairShare replace our LMS? +
No. FairShare sits alongside Moodle, Canvas, Blackboard, and other LMS systems. Your LMS handles submission and grades; FairShare handles the group-work process that LMS tools were never built for.
How does FairShare track contributions? +
Each team member logs tasks, updates, and completed work directly in the platform. FairShare aggregates this in real time to show a breakdown of who is doing what — across writing, research, coordination, and other task types. All logs are visible to the whole team.
Can someone game the system by logging fake tasks? +
Task logs are visible to the whole team and, where enabled, to the instructor too. Transparency is the accountability mechanism — it's difficult to inflate contributions without teammates noticing.
Can lecturers and tutors see team progress? +
Yes. Instructors can be given dashboard access to monitor activity, view contribution breakdowns, and track project milestones across all groups in a module — giving a far more accurate picture than a single submitted report.
Can FairShare data inform marking decisions? +
That's entirely at the instructor's and institution's discretion. FairShare provides contribution logs, activity timelines, and task completion records — how that informs marking is a decision for your module policy, not the platform.
Who can see student contributions? +
By default, contributions are visible to the student's own team. Instructor visibility is only enabled if the institution configures it. Students always have visibility into who can access their project data.
Is student data stored securely? +
FairShare is built on a Supabase backend with row-level security, real-time data handling, and secure authentication. Project data is only accessible to authorised users within a project. See our Security & trust page for full details.
